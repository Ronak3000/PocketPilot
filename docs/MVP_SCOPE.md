# PocketPilot MVP Scope

PocketPilot is a pre-spend financial decision companion.
It is NOT an expense tracker, traditional budgeting app, banking app, investment product, or payment application.

## MVP Scope
- Single-user
- Demo-first
- No authentication
- No accounts
- No sessions
- No multi-tenancy
- No Row-Level Security
- No payment initiation
- No direct banking integrations
