# AI Safety Document

**AI may:**
- Interpret natural language
- Understand Hinglish
- Read screenshots
- Extract purchase details
- Identify missing information
- Suggest candidate scenarios
- Explain verified results
- Draft Constitution rules

**AI may not:**
- Calculate final balances
- Calculate total EMI commitment
- Calculate goal delay
- Mark a plan safe without finance simulation
- Invent income or commitments
- Activate Constitution rules without approval
- Initiate payments
- Recommend securities
- Silently change deterministic outputs
